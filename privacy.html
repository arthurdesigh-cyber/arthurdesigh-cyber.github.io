Cadence — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Cadence is a personal, self-hosted publishing tool used only by its owner.

What is stored

  • The videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, hashtags and schedules you create.
  • For each connected account: the account ID, display name, avatar URL, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by the platform. Tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
  • Publishing logs (time, status, error messages) for each post.

How it is used

Data is used only to publish your videos to your own accounts at the times you choose and to show you the status of those posts. Data from TikTok (basic profile info and post status) is used only for this purpose.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to the platform you choose to publish to, through its official API.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the server you run Cadence on until you delete it. Deleting a video removes its files and schedule. Disconnecting an account deletes its stored tokens and revokes access where the platform supports it. You can also revoke access from TikTok: Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.
