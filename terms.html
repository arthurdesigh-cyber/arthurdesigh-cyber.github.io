Cadence is a personal, self-hosted tool that lets its operator plan, schedule and publish their own videos to their own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) through each platform's official API.

  1. Who can use it. Cadence is operated by a single owner for their own accounts. It is not offered as a service to the public.
  2. Your content. You keep all rights to the videos, titles, descriptions and hashtags you publish. You are responsible for making sure your content follows the rules of each platform, including TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  3. Connected accounts. You connect accounts with the platform's official login (OAuth). You can disconnect at any time from the Accounts page or from the platform's own settings.
  4. Availability. Cadence is provided "as is", without warranty. Scheduled posts may be delayed or fail if a platform's API is unavailable or rejects a post.
  5. Changes. These terms may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
